Why it works

Mass blast pays the discount to guests who would have booked anyway. The causal policy mails fewer guests and still captures 93% of the incremental bookings (825 vs 888).

How far to trust it

Counterfactuals come from synthetic data, the 2,000-points arm is modelled, and results are one seed with no significance tests between estimators.

Every figure on this slide is from the 12,501-guest held-out test set. Policy values use the ground-truth counterfactuals in the synthetic data. The incremental-booking comparison is 824.6 for the causal policy against 888.2 for mass blast.
