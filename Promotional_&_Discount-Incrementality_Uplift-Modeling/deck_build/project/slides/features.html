Stage 2 · Features and propensity

Split, propensity model and balance check

  • Stratified split by treatment × holdout: 30,000 train, 7,499 validation, 12,501 test
  • log1p and standardization for the neural nets
  • 5-fold cross-fitted GBM propensity: out-of-fold e(x) on the training set
  • Stabilized, clipped inverse-propensity weights
  • Overlap: e(x) spans 0.13–0.85, nobody below 0.1 or above 0.9
  • Max |SMD| falls from 0.537 (email opens) to 0.009 after IPW

Left: propensity overlap, treated vs control. Right: |SMD| per covariate, unweighted vs IPW-weighted.

The balance check is the main evidence that the observational selection bias is handled: before weighting, treated and control guests differ by up to half a standard deviation on email open rate, tier and recency; after weighting every covariate is within 0.01.
