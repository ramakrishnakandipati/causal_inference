Confounding by design

Always-Takers are mailed most (0.63), Lost Causes least (0.26): a naive comparison points the wrong way.

Conversion is not margin

Always-Takers barely change their odds yet cost $63 per mailed guest: discounts land on stays that happen anyway.

Persona averages are over all 50,000 generated guests. P(mailed) is the mean true mailing probability in the generator, including the randomized holdout. Persona is stored for evaluation only and never used as a model feature.
