Wasted offers fall from $40,274 to $2,020. Scaled to a 1,000,000-guest database: −$14.95M against +$7.10M.

The scaling is linear from the 2,000-guest sample, with a $0.05 send cost included, so treat the millions as an order of magnitude and not a forecast. Propensity targeting loses because it ranks by likelihood to book, which favours the Always-Takers who would have booked anyway.
