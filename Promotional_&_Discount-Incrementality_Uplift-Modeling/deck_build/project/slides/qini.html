All eleven estimators beat the baselines

Every curve climbs above the random line and tracks the oracle, which ranks by the true effect.

They peak around the top 20–35% of the list, then fall: mailing deeper destroys margin.

The propensity-to-buy baseline ends up below random, because it picks guests who book anyway.

UpliftRF is the lowest of the eleven curves.

The vertical axis is cumulative incremental margin per guest, IPW-weighted on the 12,501-guest test set. The right-hand end of every curve is the average effect of mailing everyone, which is negative.
