Model comparison · Which one to use

Pick by constraint, not by a single metric

Default: CausalForestDML

Best PEHE (14.94) and Qini (0.952 IPW, 0.949 RCT), native intervals, 2.6 s to fit. Costs 31 ms to score 1,000 guests.

Latency-critical: TARNet or R-Learner

1.4 ms per 1,000 guests. PEHE 15.14 and 15.57; TARNet has the best uplift in the top 20% ($12.25).

Uncertainty-first: LinearDML

Fastest fit (1.9 s) and best interval coverage (70.7%), but the widest intervals and the weakest ranking after UpliftRF (Qini 0.930).

Handle with care: UpliftRF

Built for conversion and mapped to margin with a plug-in: PEHE 17.49, Qini 0.907. In the campaign flow it mails 1,089 guests and earns +$11.6k.

In the 2,000-guest campaign, seven estimators land within $20 of +$14.2k. LinearDML, TARNet and DragonNet are $0.3–0.4k lower, UpliftRF $2.6k lower.

The practical conclusion is that the choice of estimator matters less than having any sound causal estimator with a sensible decision threshold: the policy value is nearly identical for seven of the eleven. The differences show up in accuracy of individual effect estimates, speed and uncertainty.
