Stage 5 · Policies and decisions

From a CATE to a CRM decision

Four CRM policies

  • Mass Blast: 20% off to 100% of guests
  • No Promo: nobody is mailed
  • Propensity Targeting: top P(Y = 1 | X), given the same mail budget as the causal policy
  • Causal Uplift: mail when τ̂ margin exceeds the $0.05 offer threshold plus the $0.05 send cost

Policy value is computed with the true counterfactuals, and checked with an IPW estimate from observed data only.
