Tabs 2 and 3: benchmark and ROI simulator

Tab 2: interactive table of all 11 estimators (Qini, AUUC, PEHE, uplift, latency) and the diagnostic plots.

Tab 3: sliders for discount %, points cost, database size and CATE threshold; net profit for each policy.

The ROI tab re-prices the discount analytically from the data-generating process, and the model side is re-priced approximately, so it is best used to explore sensitivity to the offer design and the targeting threshold rather than to forecast.
