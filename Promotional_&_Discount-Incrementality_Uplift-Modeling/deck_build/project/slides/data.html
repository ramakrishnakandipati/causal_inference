Confounded treatment

Mail probability rises with email opens, tier, price sensitivity and frequency, falls with recency, and is clipped to 0.05–0.95. A 20.3% randomized holdout is mailed with probability 0.5.

Coupled outcomes

Y(0) and Y(1) share one uniform draw. A booking earns R × 35% margin; under the offer it earns R × (35% − 20%). Truth is stored for audit: τ conv, τ margin and persona, never as a feature.
