The CRM historically mailed engaged, loyal guests. Comparing mailed with unmailed guests directly would credit the promo with engagement that was already there.

Three of the four assumptions are checked or controlled by design. SUTVA is simply assumed: there is no interference between guests, for example no capacity effect from one guest's booking on another's availability.
