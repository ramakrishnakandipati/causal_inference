Limitations

What this does not show

Synthetic data, one seed

No significance tests. The nine best differ by about 1.2 PEHE points, which a new seed could reorder.

Tuned on this data

Persona thresholds, including the 0.06 Sleeping Dog cut-off, were set after seeing a confusion matrix.

Intervals under-cover

The 95% intervals hold the true effect for 8–71% of guests: compare guests, do not read them as probabilities.

The points offer is modelled

Its effect is assumed to be 80% of the discount's conversion lift with a $20 liability, not observed.

Typical guests by construction

The four named flow guests are the most typical of their persona. The campaign flow shows how less typical guests fare.

Explanations and tools

SHAP explains a surrogate of each estimator. The ROI tab re-prices approximately. LightGBM and UpliftRF run on CPU, as does serving.

None of these change the shape of the conclusion, which is that targeting by estimated incremental effect beats mailing everyone or mailing the likely bookers. They do mean the exact dollar figures should be re-derived on real data, ideally with a randomized holdout for evaluation.
