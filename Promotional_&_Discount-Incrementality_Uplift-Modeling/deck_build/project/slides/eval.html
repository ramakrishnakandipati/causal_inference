Stage 5 · Evaluation

How the estimators are judged

PEHE

√ mean((τ̂ − τ)²) against the true effect, in dollars of 30-day margin. Lower is better. Only possible because the data is synthetic.

Qini and AUUC

Rank guests by τ̂ and track cumulative incremental margin per guest as the list grows. AUUC is the area under that curve.

Normalized Qini

(AUUC − random) ÷ (oracle − random), where the oracle ranks by the true τ. A score of 1.0 is a perfect ranking.

Uplift at K

Observed treated-minus-control margin inside the top 10%, 20% and 30% of the ranking, IPW-weighted.

Two evaluation sets

The IPW-weighted test set (12,501 guests) and the unbiased randomized holdout inside it (2,532 guests).

Policy value, CI coverage

Policy value is Σ true τ over mailed guests minus $0.05 per mail. Coverage is the share of guests whose true τ falls in the 95% interval.

Ranking metrics use observed outcomes only, so they are what a real team could compute. PEHE, policy value and interval coverage need the ground-truth counterfactuals and are only available here because the data is simulated.
